# RFQ — Celle frigorifere BOM-024

**Progetto:** Carnia TerraTech  
**Aggiornato:** 17 settembre 2026

## 1. Oggetto

Offerta completa per due celle positive indipendenti nel Tech Barn, incluse strutture, refrigerazione, porte, controlli, monitoraggio, scarichi condensa, commissioning e documentazione.

## 2. Working duty

CR-A COLD-LEAF:
- setpoint working 1–3 °C;
- adjustment 0–5 °C;
- RH elevata;
- product pull-down importante.

CR-B COOL-SENSITIVE:
- setpoint working 10–12 °C;
- adjustment 7–15 °C;
- RH 90–95%+ da crop card.

Working size: ~25 m² e 60–70 m³ per cella. Quotare anche variante dopo sizing reale.

## 3. Dati richiesti — involucro

- dimensioni ext/int;
- pannello material/thickness;
- U/lambda;
- reaction to fire;
- coating;
- food/hygiene suitability;
- joint system;
- floor construction;
- wheel load;
- door clear opening;
- emergency release;
- door closer/contact;
- impact protection;
- condensate/drains;
- assembly and sealants.

## 4. Dati richiesti — load calculation

Il fornitore deve consegnare il calcolo separando:

- transmission;
- infiltration/door;
- product sensible;
- product respiration;
- fan/lights/people;
- defrost;
- safety margin.

Dichiarare:
- Tamb/Tcond design;
- kg product per pull-down;
- T initial/final;
- pull-down hours;
- box/pallet assumptions;
- evaporating/condensing temperatures;
- capacity and power at design point.

Non accettiamo selezione basata solo su "m³ cella".

## 5. Refrigeration scenarios

Quotare per CR-A:
- 1×100%;
- 2×partial circuits con holding capability dopo single failure.

Quotare per CR-B:
- 1×100%;
- opzione 2×partial.

Preferenza:
- R290/natural;
- oppure refrigerant GWP <150, già long-term compatible.

Per ogni soluzione:
- refrigerant;
- safety class;
- charge;
- compressor;
- evaporator;
- fan type;
- TD;
- airflow;
- sound;
- defrost;
- condensate;
- rated capacity;
- electrical input;
- seasonal/part-load data;
- min/max ambient;
- IP;
- oil/maintenance;
- service access.

## 6. Humidity

Dichiarare:

- humidification option;
- water quality requirements;
- condensation risk.

## 8. Monitoring

Per cella:
- local controller;
- Modbus/RS485/Ethernet interface;
- room probes;
- evaporator probe;
- RH;
- door contact;
- independent logger;
- alarm relay;
- local horn/light;
- remote notification;
- energy meter.

Cloud must not be required for control.

## 9. Electrical / continuity

Dichiarare:

- max input;
- starting/inrush;
- breaker;
- phase;
- defrost input;
- crankcase heater;
- control input;
- generator compatibility.

Controller/logger/network on UPS; refrigeration power from grid/generator.
