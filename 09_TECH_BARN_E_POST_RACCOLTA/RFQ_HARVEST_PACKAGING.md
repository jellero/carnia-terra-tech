# RFQ — Raccolta e packaging BOM-025

**Progetto:** Carnia TerraTech  
**Aggiornato:** 17 settembre 2026

## 1. Oggetto

Richiesta di offerta separata per:

- cassette food-contact;
- carrelli/dolly;
- tavoli e stazioni;
- bilance;
- termosigillatura;
- etichettatura;
- scanning;
- optional wash/dry leafy;
- consumabili;
- ricambi;
- commissioning.

## 2. Lotto A — cassette

Richiedere almeno:

- 600×400 shallow;
- 600×400 medium/deep;
- ventilated food-contact option;
- closed food-contact option.

Dichiarare:

- material virgin/recycled;
- food-contact declaration;
- external/internal dimensions;
- weight;
- max load;
- stacking load;
- nesting;
- perforation;
- handles;
- cleaning temperature/chemicals;
- freezer/cold-room suitability;
- expected life;
- color coding;
- MOQ;
- unit price at 50/100/250/500 pcs.

## 3. Lotto B — dolly/carrelli

- 600×400 low dolly;
- ergonomic raised dolly;
- 500 kg platform cart.

Dichiarare wheel material, brakes, washability, floor marking, spare wheels and bearings.

## 4. Lotto C — workstations

Working: 3× stainless table 1800×700.

Quote:

- fixed;
- height-adjustable;
- backsplash/no backsplash;
- lower shelf;
- mobile option;
- scales/printer mounts.

Require hygienic joints and cleanable design.

## 5. Lotto D — weighing

Quote:

- 2× bench scale ~30 kg;
- 1× platform scale ~300 kg.

If offered for trade-by-weight, state legal metrology approval/verification applicable in Italy.

Provide:
- division;
- min weight;
- platform;
- IP;
- battery;
- RS232/USB/Ethernet;
- printer/data export;
- calibration/service.

## 6. Lotto E — tray sealer

Quote manual and semi-automatic separately.

Require:
- max tray;
- interchangeable dies;
- cycle/h;
- seal temperature;
- recipe storage;
- film width;
- cut system;
- CE;
- spare heaters/PTFE;
- die price per SKU;
- gas/MAP option separate.

## 7. Lotto F — label printer

Working: Zebra ZD421 class, TT 203 dpi, Ethernet + USB.

Quote:
- printer;
- spare printhead;
- platen roller;
- ribbons;
- labels paper and PP;
- peeler/cutter optional;
- 3–5 year service.

## 8. Lotto G — leafy wash/dry OPTIONAL

Do not include in baseline total unless separately identified.

Quote:

- small washer/spinner;
- 35 L electric spinner;
- 70 L electric spinner;
- industrial 6–12 kg/cycle option.

For each:
- kg/batch;
- kg/h;
- damage rate;
- water L/kg;
- electricity;
- sanitation;
- drain;
- baskets;
- spare bearings/belt;
- safety interlock;
- cleanability.

## 9. Acceptance

- crate fit through harvest/AMR/cold-room loop;
- 100 cycles cart/dolly;
- table ergonomics trial;
- scale check;
- label print/scan 500 labels;
- seal test by SKU;
- cold/condensation label adhesion;
- traceability from harvest to shipment;
- optional washer water-quality/HACCP validation;
- optional spinner damage test on real leaf.

## 10. Costing

Separate:

- CAPEX;
- install;
- consumables per 1.000 packs;
- replacement parts;
- annual service;
- labels/ribbon/film;
- trays/bags;
- energy;
- water;
- waste;
- grant-eligible vs actual cost.
