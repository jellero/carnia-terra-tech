https://www.efsa.europa.eu/it/news/new-advice-and-app-manage-process-water-fruit-vegetables-and-herbs

Finding used:
process-water quality matters; replenishment + disinfection can help maintain microbiological quality.

Detailed EFSA:
https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2025.9173

EU guidance:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=oj%3AJOC_2017_163_R_0001
